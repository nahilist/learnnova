# Learnnova AI - Chrome Extension (Manifest V3)

Official **Learnnova AI Study & Video Tutor** extension for Google Chrome.

---

## ✨ Features

1. **All-in-One Chrome Side Panel:**
   - Persistent study sidebar on the right of Chrome (`Ctrl+Shift+L` or Cmd+Shift+L).
   - Study websites, lecture slides, research papers, and YouTube videos side-by-side without leaving your tab.

2. **YouTube Video Summarizer & Instant Notes:**
   - 1-Click video summarization with full chapter timestamps.
   - Click any timestamp to jump directly to that point in the YouTube video!
   - In-page `⚡ AI Notes` pill button right inside the YouTube player action bar.

3. **Grounded AI Tutor Chat:**
   - Context-aware Socratic tutor with your active webpage or video loaded as grounding context.
   - Quick prompt chips: *"Explain simply"*, *"Key takeaways"*, *"Give real-world example"*.

4. **Practice Mode (Quizzes & Flashcards):**
   - Active recall flashcards with 3D flip animation.
   - Interactive multiple-choice quizzes with instant grading and explanations.

5. **Right-Click Context Menu:**
   - Highlight any text on any webpage -> Right-click -> **"Explain with Learnnova AI"**.
   - Right-click anywhere -> **"Summarize this page with Learnnova AI"**.

---

## 🚀 How to Install in Google Chrome (Developer Mode)

1. Open **Google Chrome**.
2. Go to `chrome://extensions` in the address bar.
3. Turn **ON** the **Developer mode** toggle (top right corner).
4. Click the **Load unpacked** button (top left corner).
5. Select the `c:\ai-tutor\extension` folder.
6. Done! Click the **Extensions puzzle icon** in Chrome's toolbar and **pin** **Learnnova AI**.

---

## ⌨️ Shortcuts & Access

- **Keyboard Shortcut:** `Ctrl + Shift + L` (Mac: `Cmd + Shift + L`) to toggle Side Panel anytime.
- **Toolbar Popup:** Click the Learnnova icon in your Chrome toolbar for quick page overview.
- **Settings:** Click the ⚙️ icon in the popup to customize the Backend API server (defaults to `http://localhost:8000`).
